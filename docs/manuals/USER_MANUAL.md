# 1. Your First Login

1. Open the website address given by your administrator.
2. Enter your work email address and password. If your company uses a Sign in with Google or Microsoft button, use the approved button.
3. If asked to choose an organization, choose the business where you are working.
4. Open your profile and change a temporary password immediately.
5. Check your name, language, theme, and availability status.

> **Remember:** Your organization name tells you which business workspace is open. Check it before sending a campaign or changing a customer record.

> **Important:** Never share your password or one-time sign-in code. An administrator does not need your password to help you.

# 3. Your Daily Routine

1. Sign in and confirm the correct organization.
2. Set yourself to Available if your team uses availability for assignments.
3. Open Chat and check unread, assigned, and waiting conversations.
4. Answer the oldest urgent customer first, unless your manager gives another priority.
5. Use approved canned responses, but read and adjust them before sending.
6. Add useful tags and notes. Transfer a conversation when another team should handle it.
7. Mark work resolved only when the customer's need is complete or the agreed next step is recorded.
8. Before logout, check that no urgent assigned conversation is waiting.

> **Good service habit:** Read the last few messages before replying. A fast answer to the wrong question creates more work.

# 4. Use Contacts

Contacts is in the Main section. Use it to find customers by name or phone number and review the information stored for that person.

1. Open Contacts from the main menu.
2. Search by customer name or full phone number.
3. Open the contact to view details, tags, assignment, conversation history, and permitted business information.
4. Correct the display name or allowed details when needed.
5. Use assignment or transfer only when your role allows it.

- Phone numbers should include the country code.
- Keep notes factual, short, and respectful.
- Do not store passwords, card numbers, government IDs, medical diagnoses, or unrelated private information in notes or metadata.
- Agents may see only contacts assigned to them; managers and administrators may see more.

> **Important:** Deleting a contact can remove associated information. Ask an administrator before deleting a real customer.

# 5. Handle WhatsApp Conversations

## Open and understand a conversation

1. Open Chat and choose the correct WhatsApp account if your business has more than one number.
2. Use the conversation list to open the customer.
3. Read the customer name, phone number, tags, assigned person, recent messages, and any internal notes.
4. Look for the customer service window. If it is closed, use an approved template to restart the conversation.

## Send a reply

1. Type a short and clear answer in the message box.
2. Use a canned response when suitable, then personalise it.
3. Attach only approved files or media and check the correct customer is open.
4. Read the complete message once before pressing Send.
5. Check the status: sent means WhatsApp accepted it; delivered means it reached the device; read means the customer opened it; failed needs attention.

## Notes, assignment, transfer, and resolution

- Internal notes are for staff and should not be written like a customer message.
- Assign the conversation when one person owns the next action.
- Transfer it to Sales, Support, Billing, or another team with a useful note.
- Resolve it when no further action is waiting. Reopen if the customer returns with an unfinished issue.

> **24-hour rule:** After a customer messages you, normal replies are usually allowed during the next 24 hours. After that window closes, use a Meta-approved template before sending free text again.

# 6. Use Canned Responses and Tags

## Canned responses

Canned responses are saved answers for common questions. Search or use the configured shortcut, insert the response, read it, update names or details, and send only when it matches the customer's question.

## Tags

Tags help the team group and find conversations, for example New Lead, Appointment, Payment Pending, Urgent, or Closed. Use the smallest number of accurate tags. Remove tags that no longer describe the customer.

> **Important:** A saved response is not automatically correct forever. Do not send old prices, hours, offers, or policies without checking them.

# 7. Templates and Campaigns

## Templates

Templates are messages approved by Meta. They are needed when your business starts a conversation or replies after the 24-hour window. A template can contain fixed words, changing values, media, and buttons.

![Templates are reviewed by Meta before they can be used.](../../public/images/11-templates.png)

*Templates are reviewed by Meta before they can be used.*

- Use the correct language and category.
- Check every changing value, such as customer name, date, amount, or appointment time.
- Never place confidential information into a template variable.
- If a template is rejected, ask the administrator to review Meta's reason.

## Campaigns

1. Open Campaigns and choose the correct WhatsApp account.
2. Choose the approved template and permitted recipient list.
3. Check the preview, variable mapping, schedule, and number of recipients.
4. Send a test to staff first.
5. Start or schedule the campaign only after approval.
6. Watch sent, delivered, read, failed, and reply results. Stop and ask for help if failures rise suddenly.

![Campaign details show progress and delivery results.](../../public/images/14-campaign-details.png)

*Campaign details show progress and delivery results.*

> **Important:** Send campaigns only to people who gave valid permission. Respect opt-outs immediately. High complaint or block rates can damage the WhatsApp account quality rating.

# 8. Chatbot, AI, and Transfers

The chatbot can answer simple questions, follow a step-by-step flow, use an approved AI context, and transfer a customer to a person.

![Chatbot overview: automatic support should always have a clear human handoff.](../../public/images/chatbot-light.png)

*Chatbot overview: automatic support should always have a clear human handoff.*

| Feature | Simple meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Keywords | A word such as HOURS or BOOK triggers a saved reply. |
| Conversation Flows | The customer follows a series of questions and choices. |
| AI Contexts | AI answers using approved instructions and knowledge. |
| Transfers | A customer moves from automation to a person or team. |

- Managers should check the transfer queue throughout the day.
- Agents should read chatbot answers and collected details before replying.
- Report wrong, unsafe, or confusing automatic answers to the administrator with the contact, time, and screenshot.
- Never rely on AI alone for diagnosis, legal advice, financial commitment, or emergency handling.

# 9. WhatsApp Flows

WhatsApp Flows are structured forms that customers complete inside WhatsApp. A business may use them for appointment requests, lead details, feedback, order information, or a guided service request.

![WhatsApp Flow builder: create short forms with a clear purpose.](../../public/images/10-whatsapp-flow-builder.png)

*WhatsApp Flow builder: create short forms with a clear purpose.*

- Use only the published flow approved for your business process.
- Explain why information is needed.
- Collect only what is necessary.
- Check the submitted details before acting.
- Escalate incomplete or suspicious submissions to a manager.

# 10. Calls

If calling is enabled, Call Logs show past calls, IVR Flows control automated menus, and Call Transfers show calls moved to staff or teams.

1. Set yourself available if required.
2. Accept only calls assigned to you or your team.
3. Confirm the customer identity before discussing private account details.
4. Use hold and transfer carefully and explain what is happening.
5. Add the required outcome or note after the call.
6. Report missing audio, dropped calls, or wrong routing with the exact time.

> **Important:** Calls may be recorded when the business enables recording. Follow your organization's notice, consent, privacy, and retention rules.

Dashboard, Agent Analytics, and Meta Insights help managers understand workload, response activity, delivery results, and account health.

- Use a date range that matches the question.
- Check volume together with response time and resolution.
- Do not judge a person from one number without checking assignments and conversation difficulty.
- Meta delivery and quality information may update later than the conversation screen.

# 12. Profile and Personal Settings

- Profile: check your full name and change your password.
- Availability status: use Available, Busy, or Offline according to team rules.
- Theme: choose light or dark for your comfort.
- Language: choose an available language you understand.
- Logout: always log out on a shared computer.

> **Access:** Settings pages appear only when your role allows them. Contact your administrator when your job changes or access is missing.

# 13. Safety and Privacy

- [ ] Confirm the customer before sharing private information.
- [ ] Send only business-approved messages and files.
- [ ] Do not request passwords, one-time codes, complete card numbers, or unrelated personal data.
- [ ] Do not paste customer information into public AI tools or personal accounts.
- [ ] Do not download customer files to a personal device unless approved.
- [ ] Report a message sent to the wrong person immediately. Do not hide the mistake.
- [ ] Lock the computer when stepping away and log out from shared devices.
- [ ] Report suspicious links, login prompts, or unexpected access changes.

# 14. Common Problems

| Problem | What you should do |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot sign in | Check the website address, email, password, and Caps Lock. Ask the administrator to confirm your account is active. |
| A menu item is missing | Your role may not include it. Ask your manager whether you need access. |
| Cannot send normal text | The 24-hour window may be closed. Choose an approved template. |
| Message failed | Check the number, account, template values, and error shown. Do not repeatedly resend. |
| Incoming message is missing | Refresh once, confirm the correct account and organization, then report the contact and time. |
| Customer is assigned to someone else | Use transfer or ask a manager. Do not work around assignment rules. |
| Template or campaign is missing | Confirm the correct organization, account, language, role, and approval status. |
| Chatbot gave a bad answer | Take a screenshot, note the exact time and customer, transfer to a person, and report it. |
| Call has no sound | Check microphone permission and headset, then report exact time and network. |
| Screen looks stuck | Wait a moment, refresh once, and avoid clicking Send repeatedly. Report what page and time it happened. |

> **A useful support message:** “At 3:15 PM in ABC Clinic, on the Chat page, customer ending 1234 did not receive my template. It showed Failed. I expected Delivered. Screenshot attached without any token or password.”
